About Me

I am currently a PhD researcher in Computing Science at the University of Glasgow, supervised by Fahim Kawsar and Tanaya Guha. My doctoral research focuses on modelling the heart–lung axis through multimodal learning for health monitoring.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my master's degree at the University of Glasgow in 2024–2025. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Islamic Azad University, Iran, where I studied from 2020 to 2024.
